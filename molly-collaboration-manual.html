A Field Guide to Collaboration
How to Work
With Molly
如何與 Molly 共事
This document exists so we don't waste time.
Read it before we begin anything together.

這份文件的存在，是為了讓我們不浪費彼此的時間。
在我們開始任何合作之前，請先讀完它。
01
Who I Am
我是誰
我是岩寬生醫的創辦人與執行長，公司第八年。我的工作是看見方向、建立架構、做決策。我不是執行者，也不打算成為執行者。
I am the founder and CEO of Acheloy Biomedicine, now in its eighth year. My role is to see the direction, build the architecture, and make decisions. I am not an operator, and I have no intention of becoming one.

我的人類圖類型是顯示者（Manifestor）。這意味著我天生的工作方式是：在內部跑完一個完整的思考與感知週期，然後直接行動或告知。我不需要共識來驅動決策。

My Human Design type is a Manifestor. This means my natural working mode is to run a complete internal sensing cycle, then act or inform. I do not require consensus to make decisions.

我的感知方式是立體的、跨維度的。當我與人互動時，我同時在閱讀對方的動機、情緒狀態、歷史模式與未來走向。這不是刻意為之，是我本來的運作方式。這代表你說的話與你底層的狀態不一致時，我會知道。
My perception is three-dimensional. When I interact with someone, I am simultaneously reading their motivations, emotional state, behavioral patterns, and likely trajectory. This is not deliberate — it is simply how I operate. Which means: if what you say and what you actually feel are misaligned, I will notice.

我的判斷更新很快。給我有邏輯、有現實基礎、有更好解法的資訊，我很願意修正自己的立場。挑戰我的方式是帶新的事實或新的角度來，不是重複你的意見或訴諸情緒。

My positions update quickly. Bring me logic, evidence, and a better solution — I will genuinely revise. The way to challenge me is with new information or a new angle, not by repeating your view or appealing to emotion.

02
How I Make Decisions
我如何做決策
我的人類圖內在權威是情緒型。這意味著我需要時間讓一個決定在我內部完整地走過一個情緒波，才能做出清晰的判斷。
My Human Design inner authority is Emotional. This means I need time for a decision to move through a complete emotional wave internally before I can reach clarity.
請這樣做 / Do This

給我時間。提前給我資訊，讓我有空間消化。當我說「我再想想」，這不是拒絕，是我的正常流程。

Give me time. Share information early so I can process. When I say "let me think about it," that is not a deflection — it is my process.

不要這樣做 / Not This

不要在當下逼我立刻給答案。不要用「你覺得呢」把決策壓回給我同時要求即時回應。這只會讓我需要更長時間。

Do not pressure me for an immediate answer in the moment. Urgency from outside disrupts my clarity — it does not accelerate it.

當我已經決定時，我會直接說，行動會很快。那個快，是因為前面我已經跑完了。

When I have decided, I will tell you directly, and movement will be fast. That speed is possible because the internal work was already done.

03
How I Communicate
我的溝通方式
我說話直接。我給你的是我真實的判斷，不是你想聽的版本。這不是不尊重，是我唯一會給你的品質。
I communicate directly. What I give you is my honest assessment, not the version you want to hear. This is not disrespect — it is the only quality I will offer.
我的思考是多線並行的，有時我說話的方式會跳躍。如果你跟不上，請問我「你現在在說哪一層？」這是有效的溝通方式。
My thinking runs on multiple threads simultaneously. My speech can jump between layers. If you lose the thread, ask: "Which layer are you on right now?" That is a valid and useful question.
我不喜歡被過度解釋或說服。如果你要改變我的想法，給我新的事實或新的角度，不要給我情緒或壓力。
I do not respond well to over-explanation or persuasion through repetition. If you want to change my mind, bring new facts or a new angle. Emotional pressure or persistence will not work.
最有效的溝通格式 / Most Effective Format

直接、清楚、有結論、有選項、有依據、有下一步。例如：「目前有 A、B、C 三個選項。我建議 B，原因是成本最低、風險可控、兩週內能驗證。」這讓我可以立刻進入決策。

Direct, clear, with a conclusion, options, rationale, and a next step. Example: "There are three options: A, B, C. I recommend B — lowest cost, manageable risk, verifiable in two weeks." This gets me into decision mode immediately.

會拖慢一切的溝通 / What Slows Everything Down

繞圈子、只丟問題不帶方案、講很多但沒結論、用模糊語言讓我猜、把你的焦慮轉移給我處理。如果你還沒整理好，請說「我需要 30 分鐘整理，再回報。」這是可以接受的。

Circling, bringing problems without solutions, talking at length without a conclusion, vague language, transferring anxiety to me. If you need time to organize, say so. That is acceptable. Dumping half-formed thoughts is not.

我說話時可能在表面談一件小事，但真正處理的是更底層的問題——品牌定位、權力結構、長期風險。如果你不確定我在哪個層次，直接問：「你現在在說哪一層？」這是有效率的溝通。

I may appear to be discussing something small on the surface while actually addressing a deeper issue — brand positioning, power structure, long-term risk. If you are not sure which level I am operating on, ask directly. It is the efficient move.

04
Energy & Working Rhythm
我的能量與工作節奏
顯示者的能量結構與薦骨型（Generator/MG）不同。我不是持續輸出的引擎。我會有高度專注、快速推進的爆發期，也需要真正的休息期，不是表面的停工。
As a Manifestor, my energy works differently from Generators. I am not a continuous-output engine. I have bursts of intense, fast-moving focus — and I require genuine rest, not just pauses between tasks.
高效合作時機 / When I'm Most Effective

方向已定、架構清楚、你來執行細節。我在前期設計架構、後期審核品質。中間的執行交給你。

能量耗損來源 / What Drains Me

重複解釋同一件事。需要管理對方情緒才能推進工作。頻繁的小問題確認。沒有必要的會議。把混亂直接丟給我處理。

最理想的合作方式：你理解方向之後，獨立執行，有重大決策點再來找我。不需要持續報告，需要清晰的節點交付。

The ideal collaboration: you understand the direction, execute independently, and bring me in at decision points — not for continuous check-ins. I need clear delivery milestones, not progress updates.

如何讓我發揮最大價值 / How to Unlock My Best Work
幫我減少雜訊。把混亂整理成選項再來找我：問題是什麼、目前狀況、三個選項、各自優缺點、你的建議、需要我決定什麼。這個格式讓我可以在最短時間給你最清楚的答案。
Reduce noise for me. Organize chaos into options before bringing it to me: what is the problem, current state, three options, pros and cons, your recommendation, what you need me to decide. This format gets you my clearest answer in the shortest time.
我很容易因為靈感太多而同時開很多線。好的合作夥伴會幫我排序，而不是讓我繼續開更多線。提醒我「這個有價值，但放進第二階段」不是否定我，是幫我。
I tend to open many threads at once when ideas are flowing. A good collaborator helps me sequence, not accumulate. Saying "this is valuable, but it belongs in phase two" is not a rejection — it is support.
05
What I Expect, By Role
不同角色，我的期待
股東
Investor /
Shareholder
你帶來的是資源，不只是資金。我需要知道你帶來什麼、你的邊界在哪、你期待介入到什麼程度。投資之後，請信任我的判斷做公司決策，帶資源進來，不要帶控制欲進來。如果你對方向有疑問，請直接說，不要繞道。

You bring resources, not just capital. I need to know what you bring, where your boundaries are, and how much involvement you expect. Post-investment: trust my judgment on operations, bring resources in, not control. If you have doubts about direction, say it directly.
合夥人
Partner
你負責你的域，我負責我的域。邊界清楚才能快速。我尊重你的專業，請你尊重我的架構。我們做的是互補，不是競爭。決策上有歧異時，我們說出來，不要累積。

You own your domain; I own mine. Clean boundaries enable speed. I respect your expertise — respect my architecture. We are complementary, not competing. If there is a disagreement, surface it. Do not let it accumulate.
外包廠商
Vendor /
Contractor
我給你清楚的方向和標準，細節由你主導。過程中有任何不確定的地方，請直接問，不要猜、不要憑感覺交付。交付前請先自我審核一次。我們彼此都有各自擅長的事，我希望你在你的專業範圍裡做到最好，我在我的範圍裡支持你。

I give you clear direction and quality standards — the execution details are yours to lead. If anything is unclear during the process, ask directly rather than guessing. Please self-review before delivery. We each have our strengths; I want you to bring your best to your scope, and I will support you in mine.
顧問
Advisor
我邀請你進來，是因為我需要你在特定範圍裡給我真實的外部視角、專業審核，或幫我打開我目前還沒有的資源與連結。這三件事你可以同時帶，也可以只帶其中一個，但請讓我清楚知道你能給什麼、你的邊界在哪。

你的角色是給建議，不是做決策。除非我正式授權，否則請不要代表我行動、對外承諾，或在未告知我的情況下推進任何事。有好的想法請直接告訴我，我會決定要不要採用、怎麼執行。

我最需要的是你講真話。不需要因為我是委託方就告訴我我想聽的。你的價值在於你看到我看不到的地方，或你願意說出我需要但不一定舒服的事。

I invite you in for honest external perspective, expert review in your domain, or to open doors I don't yet have access to — sometimes all three. Be clear with me about what you can offer and where your limits are.

Your role is advisory, not executive. Unless I formally authorize otherwise, please do not act on my behalf, make commitments externally, or advance anything without informing me first. Bring me your ideas directly — I will decide whether and how to move.

What I need most is your honesty. You don't need to tell me what I want to hear because I'm the one who invited you. Your value is in seeing what I can't see, or saying what I need to hear but might not be comfortable with.
員工
Team Member
我需要你能夠獨立判斷、獨立執行，同時清楚知道什麼事情需要讓我知道。不需要持續匯報，但需要在重要節點主動告知。犯錯沒關係，隱瞞不行。我對能力不足可以接受，對誠信問題零容忍。

I need you to execute independently and know when to escalate. No constant reporting, but proactive communication at key moments. Mistakes are fine. Concealment is not. I can work with skill gaps. I cannot work with integrity gaps.
06
Hard Lines
我的地雷
Things That End Collaborations / 會終止合作的事
  • 01
    說一套做一套。行為與言語不一致，我會感應到，信任不容易修復。
    Say one thing, do another — I feel the misalignment before I can name it, and trust does not recover easily.
  • 02
    繞路溝通。有話直說，不要透過第三方傳話，不要用迂迴方式試探我的底線。
    Go around instead of direct — come to me, not through intermediaries.
  • 03
    用情緒或壓力控制決策。你可以有不同意見，不可以用施壓的方式推翻我的判斷。
    Control through emotion or pressure — you can disagree, you cannot override.
  • 04
    隱瞞會影響我判斷的關鍵資訊。知道卻不說，是主動的失信，不是疏忽。
    Withhold information that affects my decisions — that is a breach, not an omission.
  • 05
    情緒勒索。用罪惡感或義務來要求我配合——我重視真誠，不接受綁架。
    Emotional coercion — binding me through guilt or obligation is leverage, not relationship.
  • 06
    表面支持、實際壓制。這種模式我辨識得很快，一旦感應到，我會關閉。
    Perform support while suppressing — I recognize this pattern quickly, and I will close.
  • 07
    進入核心位置卻不承擔對應責任。這不是合作，是消耗。
    Take core access without taking responsibility — that is extraction, not partnership.
07
Preferred Collaboration Structures
我偏好的合作結構
我可以信任人，但不會把核心命脈太早交出去。合作關係的深度要與實際貢獻和信任的積累相對應。
I can trust people, but I do not hand over core assets prematurely. The depth of a collaboration should match the actual contribution and accumulated trust — not just intent or enthusiasm.
適合的結構 / Structures That Work

階段性合作、KPI 解鎖、分潤制、專案制、小比例股權搭配 Vesting 機制、明確的權限切割。進入越深，責任越清楚。

Phased collaboration, KPI-unlocked equity, profit-sharing, project-based, small equity with vesting, clear authority delineation. Deeper access requires clearer accountability.

不適合的結構 / Structures That Don't

一開始就給大股、權責不清、情感式合作、口頭承諾過多、沒有退出機制、因為感動就綁死。

Large equity up front, unclear accountability, emotionally-driven agreements, excessive verbal commitments, no exit mechanism, binding decisions made on sentiment.

08
What Good Looks Like
最好的合作是什麼樣子
最好的合作狀態是：你理解我在建什麼，你相信這個方向，你在你負責的範圍裡做到最好，有問題直接來找我，我們彼此都不需要消耗能量在管理對方。
The best collaboration looks like this: you understand what I am building, you believe in the direction, you execute your scope at full quality, you come to me directly when there is a real question — and neither of us wastes energy managing the other.

我的目標不是永遠最忙的那個人。我的目標是建立一個不需要我在場才能運轉的系統，然後退回去做我最擅長的事：看見方向、研發創新、授權擴張。

My goal is not to be the busiest person in the room. My goal is to build a system that runs without my constant presence — and then step back to do what I do best: see direction, develop IP, and expand through licensing.

如果你與我合作，你選擇的是一個長期視野的合作夥伴，不是一個應急的解法。我需要你也是。
If you choose to work with me, you are choosing a long-horizon partner, not a short-term fix. I need you to be the same.
最高原則 / The One Principle

不要控制我，要協助我落地。不要壓縮我，要幫我放大。不要消耗我，要和我一起建立更高品質的系統。

Don't control me — help me land. Don't compress me — help me amplify. Don't drain me — build something higher quality with me.
